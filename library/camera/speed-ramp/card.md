# Speed-ramp push (A → 2× → 0.5× → 2× → B)

Use: Reveals, a hero flying out of a crowd, a lockup moving to the header, scene-to-scene travel, any move that should feel edited rather than mechanical; pair the fast ends with motion blur and a whoosh, the slow middle with the key word.

Avoid: Calm, sincere beats; moves under 0.5 s (the ramp cannot read); stacking two ramps back to back with no rest.

How: A push toward a point on one smooth speed curve: it eases out of rest, speeds up to about 2× the average, glides through the middle at about 0.5× (the beat the eye reads), speeds up again and settles into the end. No corners anywhere: an easing, not a hard time remap. The speed-ramp ease gives the same curve to any other move.
