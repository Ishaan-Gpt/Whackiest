# Punch in

Use: On a word boundary to mark a key word or a new thought; alternate with punch out so a single take feels like two cameras.

Avoid: Two punches inside one second; punching past ~1.4x on low-resolution footage (it goes soft).

How: An instant reframe tighter (no visible motion), aimed at a point if given; it holds until the next call. The talking-head editor's basic cut.
